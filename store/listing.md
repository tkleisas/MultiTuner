## Full description

```
MultiTuner is a fast, offline tuner, metronome, drum machine and four-track cassette recorder for stringed instruments. It shows a large note readout, a cents needle, an animated strobe and live feedback for every string or course in the selected tuning.

Built for guitar, Greek bouzouki, saz, tambouras, oud, laouto and Renaissance lute:

Guitar: Standard E, Drop D, Eb Standard, D Standard, Drop C, C Standard, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, Open E, Baritone B and 7-string Standard.
Bouzouki: 4-course C-F-A-D and D-G-B-E; 3-course D-A-D and D-G-D.
Saz: Bozuk (Kara) and Bağlama in low-A and high-A variants.
Tambouras: D-G-C (default), D-A-D and D-A.
Oud: Arabic/Greek C-F-A-D-G-C, Arabic D-G-A-D-G-C, F-A-D-G-C-F and Turkish Bolahenk.
Laouto: mainland C-G-D-A and Cretan G-D-A-E, including octave-pair courses.
Lute: Renaissance 6-, 8- and 10-course tenor lute in G.

HOW IT WORKS
Play one string at a time. MultiTuner detects the pitch with a native YIN detector, finds the nearest string or course, and shows how many cents you are away from the target. Tap a course chip to lock the tuner to that course when you want to focus on a single string. The needle, strobe and colour feedback make it easy to see when you are in tune.

METRONOME
- 30–300 BPM with tap tempo and a large tempo display
- 2, 3, 4 or 6 beats per bar
- Quarter, eighth and sixteenth subdivisions
- Accent toggle and volume control
- Sample-accurate on-device audio with a shared transport

DRUMS
- 8-track synthesized drum kit
- 16-step sequencer with normal and accent steps
- Rock, Funk, House, Break and Empty patterns
- Swing, drum volume and optional metronome click
- Drums and click share one transport and can run together

CASSETTE RECORDER
- Full-width four-track tape with four mono takes and a 15-minute limit
- Arm and record takes, then overdub while hearing the existing tracks
- Volume, constant-power pan, mute and solo on every deck strip
- Rewind and fast-forward by 10 seconds per tap
- Flip the cassette to play the same tape backwards with physical 1-4 / 2-3 routing
- Playback varispeed from 0.5x to 2x; click and drums follow the speed
- Tape emulation with saturation, wow, flutter, hiss, tone and wet/dry mix
- Reverb and delay master effects with per-deck sends and master returns
- Adjustable 0-200% record input gain, with live input and tape output meters
- Export the cassette as a forward 1x stereo mix in WAV or OGG Opus
- Share exports through the Android share sheet to Files, Drive, email or chat
- Multiple cassettes with selectable shell colours
- Recordings stay in private app storage on your device

FEATURES
- Native C++ YIN pitch detection
- Automatic string/course detection and manual course lock
- Large note, cents, confidence, needle and strobe feedback
- Adjustable A4 reference from 415 to 466 Hz
- Reverb/delay sends and returns, stereo WAV/OGG export and Android sharing
- Phone and tablet layouts
- Works offline
- No ads, no account and no internet permission
- Tuner audio is processed on-device; cassette takes are stored locally and never uploaded

For best results, play close to the microphone in a quiet room and tune one string at a time.

MultiTuner is suitable for practice, instrument setup and live tuning.
```

## Reviewer instructions

1. Open the app and grant microphone permission.
2. Select a tuning from the header.
3. Play a single string near the microphone.
4. Tap a course chip to lock a string; tap it again to return to auto mode.
5. Adjust the A4 reference with the A4 button and pause/resume with the Audio button.
6. Open Metronome from the bottom navigation; press START and verify the beat indicator and click playback.
7. Open Drums; press START and verify the step indicator and drum playback. Tap a step to cycle off → normal → accent.
8. Open Cassette; arm a deck strip, press REC and verify that the REC IN meter responds. Press SAVE to finish the take.
9. Press PLAY, then use REW/FF and FLIP to check tape navigation and reverse routing. Try varispeed and the tape-emulation controls.
10. Raise a deck REV/DLY send and the matching MASTER FX return to verify reverb and delay.
11. Tap EXPORT, choose WAV or OGG Opus, and verify that the Android share sheet opens with the rendered stereo mix.

No test account or login is required.

## Signing notes

- Play App Signing is required for new apps.
- Recommended setup: let Google generate and manage the app signing key, and use
  the existing keystore as the upload key.
- Export the upload certificate for Play Console:

  ```bash
  keytool -export -rfc \
    -keystore ~/.android/keystores/multituner-release.p12 \
    -alias multituner \
    -file ~/.android/keystores/multituner-upload-cert.pem
  ```

- Upload the resulting `.pem` certificate in Play Console under
  **Release > Setup > App signing**.
- The Play Store build is re-signed by Google. GitHub release APKs are signed
  with the upload key, so a sideloaded APK cannot be upgraded in place by the
  Play Store version; uninstall the sideloaded app before installing from Play.

## Testing and production access

If this is a **personal** developer account created after November 13, 2023,
Google requires a closed test with at least **12 testers opted in for 14
consecutive days** before you can apply for production access. Keep the testers
opted in continuously and collect feedback for the production access
application.

An **organization** account does not have this 12-tester requirement.
